Privacy Policy

Ridna Hvylia Publisher · last updated 30 October 2026

Ridna Hvylia Publisher is a private publishing tool used only by the owner of the Ridna Hvylia music brand to schedule and publish his own videos to his own social media accounts (YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, Threads).

What data we access

When the owner connects one of his own accounts, the tool receives an access token and basic profile information (account name, ID, avatar) and, where granted, statistics for the owner's own posts. No data of other users is collected.

How data is used

The data is used only to upload the owner's videos at the scheduled time and to show him statistics of his own content. It is stored locally on the owner's computer and is never sold, shared or used for advertising.

Deleting data

The owner can disconnect any account at any time in the tool and revoke access in the platform's settings; stored tokens are then deleted.

Privacy Policy

Ridna Hvylia Publisher · last updated 1 November 2026

Ridna Hvylia Publisher (Publikator) is a private publishing tool used only by the owner of the Ridna Hvylia music brand to schedule and publish his own videos to his own social media accounts (YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, Threads).

YouTube API Services

Publikator uses YouTube API Services to upload the owner's videos to his own YouTube channels and to read basic information about those channels. By using Publikator you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

YouTube data accessed: channel name, channel ID and avatar (youtube.readonly), and permission to upload videos (youtube.upload). Publikator stores only the OAuth tokens, the channel ID and the IDs of uploaded videos, locally on the owner's computer. It does not use cookies or tracking, and does not share any YouTube data with third parties.

What data we access

When the owner connects one of his own accounts, the tool receives an access token and basic profile information (account name, ID, avatar) and, where granted, statistics for the owner's own posts. No data of other users is collected.

How data is used

The data is used only to upload the owner's videos at the scheduled time and to show him statistics of his own content. It is stored locally on the owner's computer and is never sold, shared or used for advertising.

Deleting data

The owner can disconnect any account at any time in the tool; stored tokens and data for that account are then deleted immediately. Access for Google/YouTube can also be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page. Stored YouTube data is deleted no later than 30 days after access is revoked, or on request to the contact address below.

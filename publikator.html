Publikator

Ridna Hvylia Publisher

Publikator is the private publishing tool of the Ridna Hvylia music brand. It uploads the owner's own music videos to his own YouTube channels at a planned time, a few hours before the premiere, so that YouTube processing finishes and the owner can review monetization and collaborators in YouTube Studio before the video goes public.

How it works

1. The owner connects his YouTube channel with Google sign-in. 2. He adds a video, title and description and picks a date. 3. Publikator uploads it with the YouTube Data API as a scheduled video. 4. YouTube publishes it at the chosen time.

Publikator uses YouTube API Services. It runs only on the owner's computer and is not offered to other users.
